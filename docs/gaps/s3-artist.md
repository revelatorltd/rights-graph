## Decided here rather than asked

- **Switches state the artist's own claim, not the resolved answer** (G-1). The sentence under each switch says what the graph resolves and why, whenever the two differ. Before this, a switch the artist turned on snapped back off because the composition side was waiting.
- **The opt-out writes three things**: an artist-level Deny for all partners (condition "AI opt-out"), a signed "AI opt-out" attestation, and Declined (authority "AI opt-out") on every open Voice/NIL request in the artist's inbox. Turning it off revokes the first two. Requests already declined stay declined.
- **Partner switches**: on writes an artist-level Grant (allowlist, that partner) plus a signed Voice/NIL consent. Off revokes the artist's own grant and writes a Deny for that partner. A grant the label made on the artist's behalf is never revoked from here, only disputed.
- **"Limit"** is a `FormDialog` ("Allow with limits": partner, only in countries, until a date). It writes a limited grant.
- **Confirming a credit** = `setCreditState` Confirmed, a "Role confirmed" attestation at the subject's ArtistID assurance, and Approved on the matching credit-confirmation request. The tier is whatever `tierOf` returns afterwards. Paper Moons reaches T4 in the prototype; Harbour Lights stays T1 because it has not been delivered.
- **The delegate** sees Mira's app. They can decide only requests whose use is in their scope (Sync). Credits, consent, dispute, inviting and claiming are each unavailable with a reason. Their decisions record `actor: Dara Whitlock, onBehalfOf: Mira Castellanos, authority: Delegate scope …`.
- **Writer mode** adds Works (own declared share, publishers, registrations missing) and Societies. Ana has no affiliation in DE (GEMA) or the US (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC): these are flagged `registration:Missing`, and the reading opens on Missing. Memberships and registrations are invented in `data.ts`.
- **"Where you're cleared"** is filed under Consent, not as a sixth tab. It reads master ("Recording") and composition ("Song") separately on each track's Clearance facet. No commercial term appears anywhere.
- **Earnings (S3-10)** is a `WayOut` on Profile › Identity's Earnings card, linking to Revelator statements.
